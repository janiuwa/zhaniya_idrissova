Working papers

Working paper

Quantifying the Ostian economy

Proposes and contextualises new stylised facts about Ostia's economy, built on a unified dataset of road networks, buildings, urban footprint and inscriptions, and maps the reach of Ostia's trade network using the EDCS epigraphic database.
